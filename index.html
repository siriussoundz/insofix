Why Insulation Can Cause Problems

Incorrect or aging insulation can lead to damp, restricted airflow and survey issues — particularly in older or solid wall properties.

Spray foam and cavity wall insulation installed under previous schemes can sometimes cause more harm than expected, especially where ventilation and moisture control are affected.
